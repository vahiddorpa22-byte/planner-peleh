On 2026-10-26 Vahid said our hooks were «خیلی خیلی ضعیف». The audit (`peleh_hook_audit_2026-10-26.pdf`) confirmed it:
- 3-second hold fell from 47.4% (first 10 Reels) to 37.6% (last 5).
- Hooks about the app, the challenge or a date averaged 38%; life-centred hooks averaged 47%.
- 8 of 15 hooks were questions, and the topic had narrowed to books only.

Rules from now on:
- Frame 0 is a real scene (hands, objects, screen), never a static navy text card. The navy template is for grid covers only.
- The hook text is on screen from 0 s: at most 7 words, about 90–110 px.
- The narrator's first word is the hook's number or keyword.
- At most one question hook per week. Rotate formats: contrast, numbers, confession, "translation" humour, POV.
- No app or challenge name in the first 3 s.
- Write 3 hook options per piece and pick one with a 5-question checklist. Broaden topics beyond books (phone, study, exercise, procrastination).

2026-10-26: Vahid approved ALL the audit rewrites and the new cover style (keep the template, add a small scene photo from the Reel). He told every unit that growing the page is the top priority, to be done with maximum care and the best quality. Ethical means only.

**Why:** retention and growth stalled, and the founder is unhappy.
**How to apply:** every Peleh Reel or carousel goes through these rules. The hook bank is in `peleh_work/hooks/hook_audit_2026-10-26.md`. Related: [[peleh-content-preferences]].
