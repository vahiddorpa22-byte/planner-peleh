Peleh (پله) is an Iranian app for daily habits, reading (30-day book challenges, monthly leaderboard), and health. Founder: Vahid (the user), who signs DMs «وحید، پله». Web app: app.pelehapp.ir; Android via Cafe Bazaar. Positioning (confirmed): «جایی که کارهات رو نصفه ول نمی‌کنی». Confirmed features are F01–F12 in the calendar workbook's `_plan` sheet, plus «۷ روز پریمیوم رایگان» (confirmed active, 2026-10-24).

Goal: 60,000 Instagram followers in the first 12 months. Treat it as a target, never a promise. Starting point on 2026-10-24: 126 followers.

The user wants Claude to act as the whole company, organised into 8 units (set up 2026-10-24):
1. Strategy & product management: goals, priorities, revenue model, growth plan, coordinating the units.
2. Advertising & campaigns: campaigns, paid ads, installs, ad budget, ROI.
3. Growth marketing: acquisition funnel, installs, visitor→user conversion, growth experiments, revenue.
4. Social media & content: Instagram, Threads, Telegram, content calendar, posts/Stories/Reels, brand voice. Has three sub-teams: graphics, editing, and scenario/ideation.
5. Community & customer relations: support, feedback, book challenges, loyalty, win-back of inactive users.
6. Software development & infrastructure: code, backend, DB, API, security, servers, payments, releases, bugs.
7. Product design & UX: UI, signup, user flows, simplifying features, completion rates.
8. Data analysis & BI: active users, retention, churn, challenge completion, subscription conversion, campaign performance.

**Why:** the user wants one place Claude can recall "where we are" whenever they ask about Peleh.
**How to apply:** when the user mentions Peleh, start from [[peleh-status]], [[peleh-decisions]], [[peleh-content-preferences]] and [[peleh-resources]]. Route work to the unit it belongs to, and say which unit is doing it. For Instagram work, use the peleh-growth-strategist skill.
